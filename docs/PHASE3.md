# Phase 3 — Money and people

Built in slices like Phases 1 and 2; each ends with end-to-end tests, a browser check, docs and a local commit. Everything is
tested locally first, and payment and other outside services are tested against local stand-ins until a real account is connected.

Tax handling follows the common Indian GST invoice format. Have your CA confirm the invoice layout, rates and registers before relying on them for filing.

| Slice | Scope | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 3.1 Invoices and GST | Billing profile per business line (GSTIN, state, address, invoice series), GST invoices from a quote or from scratch, CGST + SGST or IGST by place of supply, consecutive numbers per financial year, issue and cancel with a reason, printable invoice, payments against invoices, sales register export. | Done (51 e2e checks, plus unit tests) |
| 3.2 Payment links | Razorpay payment links on invoices, signed webhook records the payment once, numbered receipts. A real Razorpay test account is not yet checked. | Done (40 e2e checks against a local stand-in) |
| 3.3 Collections and ledgers | Customer ledger with running balance, money due by age, instalment plans that move the due date, reminders to the owner and optional customer email, download. Payments not tied to an invoice show on the ledger as received. | Done (36 e2e checks, plus unit tests) |
| 3.4 Expenses and P&L | Expenses by category and business line, or organization-wide; profit and loss by month, business line and category, on invoiced or received income, with downloads. A guide for running the business: salaries come in by hand until payroll exists (Phase 4), and bills cannot be attached yet. | Done (40 e2e checks) |
| 3.5 Packages and access | Public gallery per business line with photos, offers, brochure links and enquiries that become leads, shareable on WhatsApp from a lead. Course, software and service access with validity, encrypted licence keys, renewal and revoking, automatic renewal leads and expiry reminders. Access is given by hand, not yet on payment. Brochures are links, not uploads. | Done (64 e2e checks) |
| 3.6 HR core | Employee records with or without a UBOS login, joining and leaving checklists, encrypted documents, PAN, bank and salary, office assets with issue and return, and daily HR reminders (birthdays, anniversaries, probation, documents running out). Employees do not see or edit their own record yet (self-service comes with attendance). | Done (57 e2e checks) |
| 3.7 Attendance and leave | Check in and out from the web (optional location), shift with late marks, weekly offs including 2nd/4th Saturdays, holidays, monthly calendar per person, corrections asked by staff and approved by their manager, HR fixes with audit, leave types (CL, SL, EL with monthly build-up and carry forward, LWP) with pro-rated balances, half days, approvals, HR-recorded leave and balance adjustments, and a monthly register with payable and loss-of-pay days (CSV) for payroll. Not yet: check-in from the Android app, office geofence, shifts per person, payroll itself. | Done (78 e2e checks) |
| 3.8a Trials and renewals | Self-serve trial signup at /signup (emailed 6-digit code, organization code check, business lines), trial and paid periods that move to grace days and then pause on their own (a paused account can only sign in, see notifications and pay; nothing is deleted; your own company never pauses), admin reminders 7, 3 and 1 day before, at the end and when paused, daily usage snapshots (users, storage, calls, messages) on Billing and in the console, console settings for signup, trial length, licences and grace days, and hand changes to a subscription with a reason. | Done (39 e2e checks) |
| 3.8b Online payment and UBOS invoices | Pay orders online through the platform Razorpay account, GST tax invoices for UBOS sales. | Planned |
| 3.9 Visual flow builder | Drawing automation as a flowchart on top of the existing rules. | Planned |
