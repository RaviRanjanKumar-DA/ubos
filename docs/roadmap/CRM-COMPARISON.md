# UBOS against the CRM market

Checked 9 Nov 2026 against the public feature and pricing pages of eight CRMs. India comes first because that is where UBOS sells.

**CRMs:** T TeleCRM · L LeadSquared · Z Zoho CRM · F Freshsales · H HubSpot · S Salesforce · P Pipedrive · M monday CRM

**Limits of this check.** A letter means the feature was advertised on the pages read. A missing letter does not prove the CRM lacks it. LeadSquared's features address returned only its homepage, and Zoho's page does not cover calling, messaging, mobile or integrations, so those two are under-counted. No CRM has every feature, and these eight are not the whole market, so review this list once a year.

**Status:** Built = working in UBOS today · Part = some of it works · Planned = already on the road map · Gap = new, added by this check.
**Priority for India:** Must · Should · Later.
